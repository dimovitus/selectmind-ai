# Privacy Policy — SelectMind AI

**Last updated:** July 22, 2026

SelectMind AI is a Chrome extension for AI-assisted reading and writing on web pages.
**We do not collect your data on our own servers.**

## What stays on your device

- Settings, custom actions, and pipelines
- Chat history (IndexedDB)
- API keys, encrypted with Web Crypto in Chrome local storage

## What leaves your device

Only when **you** run an AI action, your browser sends a request **directly** to the AI provider you configured (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Ollama, etc.). That may include selected text, page title, URL, and conversation context.

## What we do not do

- No analytics or advertising
- No SelectMind AI backend that stores prompts or keys
- No sale of user data

## Donations

Optional Privat24 donation links open outside the extension. Payment data is not processed by the extension.
